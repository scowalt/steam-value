## P2 — cancellation crossed sequence/completion awaits

Formerly a signal checked before sequence work could become aborted during the actual sequence-read reply or final native write, yet dispatch or publication still returned imported/complete. Cancellation during final inventory or complete-receipt persistence likewise lacked a closing check.

`src/db/transfer.ts` checks the public signal after sequence-phase persistence/read, immediately before mutation after real statement preparation, after acknowledged native groups, after progress persistence, after final inventory and after complete-receipt persistence. Known row acknowledgements update counters before cancellation is raised; lost replies still never claim an acknowledgement. Verified table counters start at zero so unwritten tables retain conservative progress evidence even when interruption now stops earlier. This preserves the original retained cancellation assertion that the next table has zero confirmed rows.

Completion persists a separate proposed complete receipt rather than overwriting the conservative in-flight outcome. Cancellation/error during that awaited publication therefore rewrites incomplete/failed evidence with the original not-started or partial-or-unknown outcome. No target data/sequence is reset, rolled back, retried or replayed by this change.

Native tests cover cancellation after a real sequence read (no subsequent dispatch), after actual preparation/arming, after a committed sequence-only write, after final inventory on empty/sequence-only targets, and at acknowledged public receipt publication. The original publication watcher trigger was unordered and is replaced only by the explicitly approved real-writer seam below. Nonempty legacy transfer cancellation retains confirmed rows and full typed contents. These are public native/transfer/artifact seams; tests do not inspect private SQL strings or functions. Validation always refuses imports cancelled before completion. A partial committed sequence is preserved in reconciliation-required recovery, cannot append/replay, and validates only after a fresh explicit import through the actual bundled Worker/HTTP/read-only behavior and native allocation proof.
